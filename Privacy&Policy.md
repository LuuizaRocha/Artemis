## 1. INTRODUCTION

ARTEMIS is an independent research platform dedicated to exploring the relationships between art, mathematics, and color science.

This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is collected, processed, stored, used, and protected when individuals access or use ARTEMIS.

The platform is independently operated by Luiza Rocha, who is responsible for determining the purposes and means of personal data processing carried out by ARTEMIS, subject to applicable law.

This Policy applies to the ARTEMIS website, account registration, analytical tools, public comments, and other services made available through the platform.

By using ARTEMIS, users acknowledge that they have been informed of the practices described in this document.

## 2. AGE RESTRICTION

ARTEMIS is exclusively intended for individuals aged eighteen (18) or older.

The platform does not knowingly collect personal data from individuals under eighteen.

By creating an account or accessing the platform, users confirm that they meet the minimum age requirement.

If ARTEMIS becomes aware that an account belongs to an individual under eighteen, the account may be suspended and the associated personal data will be reviewed for deletion, subject to applicable legal obligations.

Anyone who believes that an individual under eighteen has registered with ARTEMIS may report the matter through the official contact address.

## 3. PERSONAL DATA WE COLLECT

ARTEMIS may collect and process the following categories of personal data.

### 3.1. Account Information

When creating an account, users may be required to provide:

- Name or display name;
- Email address;
- Username;
- Password.

These details are used to create, identify, and manage individual accounts.

Passwords are authentication credentials and are not intended to be displayed publicly.

### 3.2. User-Generated Content

When a registered user publishes a comment associated with an artwork, ARTEMIS processes:

- The written comment;
- The selected public display name;
- The association between the comment and the relevant artwork;
- Technical information necessary to manage the publication.

Comments are publicly visible while the associated account and publication remain active.

### 3.3. Technical and Usage Information

ARTEMIS may automatically collect technical information when users access the platform, including:

- IP addresses;
- Browser information;
- Device and operating system information;
- Access timestamps;
- Technical logs;
- Cookie identifiers;
- Information about interactions with platform features.

Such information may be processed for security, troubleshooting, technical maintenance, and usage analysis.

### 3.4. Communications

If users contact ARTEMIS through its official email address, information included in their messages and any necessary correspondence may be processed to respond to their requests.

Users should avoid including unnecessary sensitive personal information in communications.

## 9. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION

ARTEMIS retains personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Policy or as required or permitted by applicable law.

Account information is retained while the account remains active.

### 9.3. Technical Logs

Technical and security logs may be retained for the periods necessary to maintain platform security, investigate incidents, comply with applicable legal requirements, and protect the rights of ARTEMIS and its users.

### 9.4. Legal Retention

Certain information may be retained after an account deletion when necessary to comply with legal or regulatory obligations, exercise legal rights, or satisfy another lawful retention basis.

Deletion from the active platform may not immediately remove information from all technical backups, temporary storage systems, or external caches.

ARTEMIS will take reasonable steps to ensure that deleted information is removed from its systems according to applicable technical and legal requirements.
