## Overview

**Artemis** investigates how patterns, geometry, proportions, and visual systems shape the way we create, observe, and interpret the world. 

Rather than treating science and art as separate domains, the project acts as an open environment for research, visual experimentation, and interdisciplinary inquiry—bridging the gap between natural structures and human creativity.

---

## The Three Pillars

| Discipline | Role in Artemis |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Mathematics** | The language of structures, proportions, and hidden patterns found in both nature and composition. |
| **Art** | The medium that translates abstract systems and observations into visual, sensory experiences. |
| **Perception** | The subjective lens through which individuals interpret, feel, and assign meaning to what they see. |

> *"Art can be studied through mathematical structures. Mathematics can inspire artistic expression. Yet neither fully explains how a person experiences a work of art."*

---

## Guiding Philosophy

> **Knowledge becomes richer when different disciplines meet.**

Artemis is built on intellectual curiosity and accessibility. Complex ideas shouldn't be locked behind academic or technical barriers; the project aims to make the intersection of logic and aesthetics welcoming to anyone willing to observe closely.

---

## Etymology & Symbolism

Named after **Artemis**—the Greek goddess of the wilderness, the moon, and the hunt—the project draws its identity from the harmony between nature, observation, and discovery:

* **The Moon:** Represents cycles, rhythm, and quiet observation.
* **The Deer:** Symbolizes organic forms and the intricate, fractals-like geometry of the natural world.
* **Mathematics:** Serves as the lens used to decode those underlying patterns.

---

## Status

Founded in **2026**, Artemis is a long-term, evolving archive of essays, visual studies, and creative experiments.
